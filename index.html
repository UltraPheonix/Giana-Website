Combining talent with truth

Giana Ribeiro is a trained vocalist who has been performing at venues across the greater Boston area for 13 years. She specializes in jazz, classical, liturgical and pop genres. Whether providing the ambience for a cocktail hour or leading a congregation in worship, Giana creates a space where the beauty of music is tangible.
